What happens to your reviews

  1. Collect

    Muse pulls reviews from the product page’s Okendo widget, or uses the ones you paste.

  2. Read

    VADER scores sentiment, TF-IDF finds the phrases people repeat, and k-means groups reviews into themes.

  3. Write

    Llama 3.1 on Groq drafts the script from those findings. You see both, side by side, so you can check every claim against a real review.
